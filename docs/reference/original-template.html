How an engagement runs

Most projects take eight to fourteen weeks. You can stop after discovery and keep everything we have produced.

  1. Discover

    Weeks 1 to 2

    Interviews, shadowing and system walkthroughs with the people who do the work.

  2. Map

    Weeks 2 to 4

    As-is process maps with measured time, handoffs and failure points.

  3. Prioritise

    Week 5

    Each fix is costed and ranked by value against effort, with your leadership team.

  4. Specify

    Weeks 6 to 10

    To-be process, requirements and acceptance criteria your build team can start on.

  5. Measure

    Day 90

    We return to check the numbers moved, and fix what did not.
